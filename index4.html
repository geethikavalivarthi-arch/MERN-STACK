Chicken Fried Rice

A simple and delicious chicken fried rice recipe.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of cooked rice
  • 200 grams of chicken
  • onions
  • spices
  • sauces
  • salt and pepper
  • 3 tablespoons of butter

Instructions

  1. chop the onions
  2. heat butter in a pan
  3. add chicken and choped onions and cook
  4. add some salt and sauces to it
  5. add some rice and spices to the rice
  6. mix well,cook it for 5 mins
  7. Then surve it and enjoy
